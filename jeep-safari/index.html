The route

What you'll see on the safari

Every trip is planned around your group and the weather. These are the places most guests ask for.

01

Amappara · Tortoise Rock

The classic off-road route to the giant tortoise-shaped boulder on the ridge, with wide views across the hills.

02

Windmill ridges

Bump along red-earth tracks between the wind turbines on the high, breezy grasslands above Ramakkalmedu.

03

Ramakkal rock viewpoint

Stand above the patchwork farms of Tamil Nadu, thousands of feet below the edge of the Western Ghats.

04

Off-road trails & hilltops

Green hills, rocky climbs and quiet viewpoints that are hard to reach without a 4×4.
